A FEW ANSWERS

This deserves an explanation.

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is the link that lets ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT act on Zak. You make your request in ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT, then Zak’s MCP server applies the settings to the browser.

What can you customize from ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT?

You can ask, for instance, to change the colors, adapt the layout of the interface or add sites to your favorites. The demo on this page shows these three uses.

Do you need ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT to browse with Zak?

No. Zak is a browser you can use as usual. Connecting it to ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT is one more way to customize it, when you feel like it.

Does the demo change my browser?

No. It is a simulation inside this page. It does not connect to your ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT account and changes no setting on your computer. It simply shows you what is possible.

How do I start customizing Zak with ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT?

First connect ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT to Zak’s MCP server. Once the connection is set up, describe the change you want in ClaudeChatGPTClaude or ChatGPT. You can start with one of the examples on this page.
